# Reel Scripts for @buffbrahmin

A working bank of Reel concepts written for the DesiFit Method audience: South Asians who want to build muscle, lose fat, and protect long-term health without giving up rice, roti, dal, or ghee.

**Voice rules:**
- First person. Direct. Short sentences.
- One myth or one tip per Reel. Don't overload.
- Hook in the first 2 seconds or scroll wins.
- End with a save/share trigger ("save this for shaadi season") or a soft CTA ("DM me 'DESI'").
- No corporate-speak. Sound like you'd sound to a cousin.

**Posting cadence (recommendation):**
- 1 talking-head educational per week (30–45s)
- 2 Desi tip reels per week (15–25s)
- 1 skit / POV / candid per week (10–30s)

**Hashtag base set:**
`#buffbrahmin #desifitness #southasianfitness #indianfitness #browngains #desidiet #vegetarianprotein #brownmuscle #desifitcoach #southasianmuscle`
