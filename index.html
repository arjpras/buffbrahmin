Why this exists

Most fitness advice wasn't built for us.

It ignores long workdays, family meals, daawats, and the simple fact that cutting out rice and roti isn't realistic. South Asians also face higher genetic risk for cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, and "skinny-fat" body composition — issues that generic Western programs don't address.

Vision & Mission

Stronger and healthier — the Desi way.

We get the hustle and bustle of South Asian culture. Our schedules, our family expectations, and our food traditions seem to be at odds with our health goals. They don't have to be.

Our Vision

A South Asian community that no longer trades culture for health. Where dal, rice, and chai coexist with strong, lean, energetic bodies — and where wellness is something we pass down, not something we sacrifice for.

Our Mission

Deliver science-backed, personalized coaching that respects Desi metabolic, cultural, and lifestyle realities. We help you build muscle, lose fat, and improve gut health — using simple, sustainable changes that fit your traditions, not fight them.

The Dal Upgrade

Standard dal serving (~7–9g protein)

→

Dal + Greek yogurt on the side

+10–15g protein per meal · same comfort, double the satiety

🧀

Paneer, Rebuilt

Restaurant-style paneer (heavy ghee/oil)

→

Grilled or air-fried paneer

Same 14g protein per 100g · ~200 fewer calories

🥛

Dahi, Doubled

Regular curd (~6–8g protein per cup)

→

Greek yogurt or chenna

+8–10g protein · better gut bacteria · less lactose

🥜

The Snack Fix

Bhajji, samosa, biscuits

→

Roasted chana, boiled eggs, or whey

+12–25g protein · fewer cravings later

🫓

Roti, Completed

Roti + sabzi alone (~5–7g protein)

→

Roti + dal + paneer/eggs/yogurt

Common Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I have to give up rice, roti, and ghee?
No — that's exactly the point. The DesiFit Method is built around the foods you grew up with. We optimize portions, timing, and pairings rather than eliminating staples. Rice, roti, ghee, dal, and dosa all stay on the menu — we just upgrade what's around them.
I'm vegetarian. Will I get enough protein?
Absolutely. We've coached lifelong vegetarians to gain meaningful muscle without ever touching meat. Greek yogurt, paneer, eggs (if you eat them), tofu, dal-yogurt combos, roasted chana, and whey protein make 100–150g of daily protein very achievable on a Desi vegetarian plate.
I'm in my 40s/50s and starting from scratch. Is this for me?
Especially for you. South Asians have higher genetic risk for type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and "skinny-fat" body composition — and resistance training plus protein-forward eating is the single most effective intervention. We program for joint-friendly, time-efficient training that meets you exactly where you are.
What equipment do I need?
Tell us what you have access to — a full gym, a basic home setup, or just dumbbells. Programs are built around your equipment, not the other way around.
How is this different from a generic fitness app?
Generic apps weren't built for our metabolism, food culture, or lifestyle. The DesiFit Method addresses higher rates of insulin resistance, central adiposity, and digestive sensitivity head-on — and works with daawats, family dinners, and home-cooked meals instead of pretending they don't exist.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract. We also offer a full refund within 1 week of purchase or after your first consultation — no questions asked.
