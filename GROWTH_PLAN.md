# DesiFit Method: Growth Plan

How to go from zero to your first 5, then 20, then 50 paying clients without a marketing budget. Built for a one-person, college-age operation.

## The math of getting paid

Your pricing (already set):
- Free Starter Kit: lead magnet, $0
- Essentials: $129/mo
- Premium 1:1: $249/mo

**Realistic first-year targets:**

| Milestone | Mix | MRR | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| First paying client | 1 × Essentials | $129 | Month 1 to 3 |
| 5 clients | 4 × $129 + 1 × $249 | $765 | Month 3 to 6 |
| 10 clients | 7 × $129 + 3 × $249 | $1,650 | Month 6 to 9 |
| 20 clients | 13 × $129 + 7 × $249 | $3,420 | Month 9 to 12 |
| 30 clients | 18 × $129 + 12 × $249 | $5,310 | Month 12 to 18 |

At 30 clients you're doing real income on the side of school. At 50 you can think about going full-time.

The **conversion math** for a fitness coach in our niche looks roughly like:
- 100 engaged Instagram followers → 1 to 3 DMs / free-consult bookings per month
- 1 in 3 free consults converts to a paying client
- So to land your first 5 clients, you probably need **~1,500 to 3,000 engaged followers** OR aggressive direct outreach.

Don't chase vanity follower count. 1,000 engaged Desis is worth 50,000 random fitness fans.

## Weeks 2 to 4: prime the engine

### Get 3 free testimonials
Offer free 6-week coaching to 2 to 3 friends/cousins in your network who are Desi and want to get in shape. Conditions:
- They commit to weekly check-ins (otherwise no point)
- You can film their before/after for content
- They give a video testimonial at the end

This is the **single highest-leverage move** you can make. One real-results post outconverts 50 Reels.

### Start the DM game
Every time someone engages with your content:
- Likes 3+ Reels in a week
- Comments meaningfully (not just emoji)
- Shares your post to their story

Send a low-pressure DM: *"Hey, saw you've been engaging with my stuff. Anything specific you're trying to figure out with your fitness right now? Happy to give some quick advice."*

Don't pitch. Just talk. People will ask about coaching themselves once they realize you're competent and approachable.

### Engage in Desi fitness communities
- Reddit: r/SouthAsianMasculinity, r/IndianHistory (oddly fitness-adjacent), r/Fitness for crossovers
- Discord: search "Desi fitness" / "Indian gym"
- Twitter/X: follow and engage with Desi fitness creators (not other coaches, but big creators in our space)

Don't shill. Help. Drop value. Let people find you.

## Months 2 to 6: build the flywheel

### Content cadence
Sustainable target:
- 4 Reels/week (use the script bank, batch-film once a week)
- 1 carousel post/week (long-form education)
- 2 to 3 Stories/day (BTS, polls, food shots)
- 1 newsletter/week to email list

That's roughly 2 hours/day of content work. Doable around school.

### Free consult flow
When someone books a Calendly call, the script should be:
1. **Listen first** (5 min): "What got you to book this? What's been hardest?"
2. **Reflect back** (5 min): "OK so what I'm hearing is X, Y, Z."
3. **Show the path** (10 min): "Here's how I'd approach that. Step 1 you'd do this. Step 2..."
4. **Pitch the offer** (5 min): "If you want me to actually walk you through this, my Essentials plan is $129/mo. It's month-to-month, full refund first week."
5. **Don't push** (5 min): "Take a few days, see how this advice lands. If it does, the Starter Kit link is below."

The "don't push" close converts 2 to 3x better than hard closes for our community. Desis are skeptical of hard sells. Earn the trust.

### Referral mechanic
Once you have 5 clients, tell them:
> "If you refer someone who signs up, you get a month free. They get 20% off their first month."

A two-sided referral converts more than a one-sided one. Most coaches forget this.

## Months 6 to 12: scale what works

### Add a group tier
Once you have ~15 to 20 clients, you'll hit the wall of 1:1 capacity. Add:
- **DesiFit Crew**: $59/mo group coaching, weekly group call, shared meal plans, community Slack.

This lets you sell to people who can't afford $129 and frees you from 1:1 hours for top earners.

### Build a content moat
- Start a YouTube channel: long-form versions of your Reels.
- Build a recipe library on your site (just blog posts, no fancy stack needed).
- One viral post on YouTube = months of leads.

### Niche down further if needed
If at 6 months you're under 5 clients, the niche might be too broad. Possible sub-niches:
- South Asian women's fitness (PCOS, postpartum is huge)
- South Asian dad bod fixers (40s/50s, T2D risk)
- South Asian college students (cheaper price point, group-only)
- Vegetarian-only South Asian fitness

Don't niche down until you have data showing the broad approach isn't working.

## The 4 mistakes that kill new fitness coaches

1. **Building features instead of selling.** You did the website. Now stop tweaking it. Sell.
2. **Posting once, expecting magic.** Volume + time. The first 30 Reels get nobody. The 50th one might pop.
3. **Refusing to do free work.** Free coaching for testimonials > paid coaching at undermarket rates.
4. **Selling on Reels.** Reels build trust. DMs and Calendly close. Don't try to close in a caption.
