Pricing FAQ

Common questions about plans.

Why is there no contract?
Sustainable habits take time, but you shouldn't be locked in. All plans are month-to-month, and we'd rather earn your subscription each month with real results than trap you in a year-long commitment.
What's the difference between Essentials and Premium 1:1?
Essentials gives you a fully customized program with weekly check-ins and a monthly review call — perfect if you're self-driven and need a smart plan. Premium 1:1 adds direct WhatsApp access to your coach, weekly program tweaks, habit coaching, bloodwork interpretation, and a family meal-plan add-on. It's built for people who want hands-on accountability and are using fitness as part of a bigger longevity strategy.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes, anytime. Most clients start on Essentials, then upgrade to Premium when life gets busy and accountability becomes the bottleneck.
What if I have a medical condition like diabetes or high cholesterol?
South Asian-specific health risks (insulin resistance, fatty liver, cardiovascular markers) are something we plan around constantly. Bring your numbers to your free consultation — if your condition needs medical supervision, we'll tell you honestly. Coaching always works alongside your physician, not instead of them.
How do I pay?
All major credit cards and PayPal. Annual plans get a 20% discount and are paid upfront. Monthly plans renew automatically and can be canceled anytime from your account.
